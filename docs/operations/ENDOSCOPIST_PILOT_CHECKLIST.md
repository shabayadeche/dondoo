# Endoscopist pilot checklist

Use this checklist with at least three endoscopists across desktop and tablet
lists before production release.

## Measure

- Time from sign-in to opening the correct patient
- Time from procedure end to a ready-for-sign-off report
- Percentage of reports missing required fields on first preview
- Number of corrections after sign-off
- Percentage of lesions with linked specimen and image references
- Follow-up tasks closed by the due date
- Autosave failures, reloads, and offline interruptions

## Observe

- Can the endoscopist find a past patient record in under 30 seconds?
- Can they complete identity, consent, team pause, and safety checks without help?
- Can they capture a lesion, image, specimen, and follow-up task as one chain?
- Do they understand why sign-off is blocked?
- Does the tablet layout work with gloves and interruptions?

## Exit criteria

- No patient-identity or wrong-facility incidents
- 95% of test reports pass preview without support after training
- Every critical quality field is visible and traceable in the PDF
- No data loss during simulated interruption or reconnect
- Endoscopists rate the workflow usable without relying on hidden help text
